Pisces LED Light Up Sign

As a project for my engineering and electricity class, I designed and built this LED Light Up Sign based on my zodiac sign. The electrical layout was designed on TinkerCAD and the electrical components were hand-soldered onto a PCB. In addition, the base, the battery cover, the acrylic sign, and the acrylic sign holder was designed on OnShape and Adobe Illustrator. The project was then assembled with various machines and hand tools (such as a CNC Machine and Laser Engraver).
